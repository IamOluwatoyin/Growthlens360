## GrowthLens 360

GrowthLens 360 is an AI-assisted business diagnostic platform designed for small businesses, including artisans, retailers and service providers in Nigeria.

The platform helps business owners understand how their view of the business compares with the experiences of their customers and, where applicable, employees. It transforms structured assessment responses into clear business insights, practical recommendations and trackable action plans.

### The problem

Many small-business owners rely on personal judgement when evaluating their operations. Customers and employees may experience the same business differently, but those perspectives are often not collected or compared in a structured way.

Traditional business-consulting tools can also be expensive, overly technical or poorly suited to businesses with limited time, staff, money and digital experience.

GrowthLens 360 makes business diagnosis more accessible by using short mobile-friendly assessments, plain language and affordable improvement actions.

### Core features

- Secure owner authentication and guided business onboarding
- Two-perspective assessments for owners and customers
- Three-perspective assessments for owners, customers and employees
- Unique private assessment links for every participant
- Email, WhatsApp and manually shared participant invitations
- Response-progress tracking and recommended response targets
- Deterministic scoring across people, operations, customer experience and digital readiness
- Perspective-gap identification and priority classification
- AI-assisted business diagnosis using supplied assessment scores
- Business insight reports with strengths, gaps and priority areas
- Practical recommendations suited to small-business realities
- Editable action plans with statuses, dates and notes
- Growth Advisor for report-based, plain-language guidance
- In-app and email notifications
- Automated participant invitations, report alerts, reminders and support routing
- Responsive desktop and mobile interfaces

### How it works

1. The business owner creates an account and completes onboarding.
2. The owner completes an eight-question self-assessment.
3. The owner invites customers and, when applicable, employees.
4. Each participant responds through a unique private link.
5. GrowthLens calculates deterministic area and metric scores.
6. AI interprets the supplied scores without changing or inventing them.
7. The owner receives an insight report, recommendations and an editable action plan.
8. The Growth Advisor answers follow-up questions using the business’s latest report.

### Product principles

GrowthLens 360 is built around accessibility, clarity and local relevance. Recommendations use supportive language and prioritise practical actions that small-business owners can begin within 7 to 30 days using familiar tools such as phone calls, WhatsApp, notebooks and simple spreadsheets.

The platform does not describe businesses as failing. Instead, it presents score differences and perspective gaps as opportunities for focused improvement.
